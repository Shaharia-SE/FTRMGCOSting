Formula Guide

Material cost / pc: Base consumption × (1 + wastage% / 100) × unit price, unit conversion অনুযায়ী।

Sweater yarn: Yarn grams per garment × (1 + wastage% / 100) ÷ 1000 × price per kg।

Trim cost / pc: Qty per garment × (1 + wastage% / 100) × unit price।

Production cost: Material/yarn + trims + all manufacturing/process costs।

Overhead: Production cost × overhead %।

Profit margin method: Base cost ÷ (1 − margin %)।

Markup method: Base cost × (1 + markup %)।

Commission gross-up: (base selling price) ÷ (1 − commission %)।

এই prototype-এ fabric/yarn unit price conversion: gram→kg, meter→meter, yard→yard, pcs→pcs, kg→kg। Price Unit-এর সঙ্গে consumption unit মিলিয়ে input দিন। For example, consumption in kg with price per kg, consumption in meter with price per meter. Sweater yarn-এর জন্য consumption unit g এবং price unit kg বেছে নিন।
